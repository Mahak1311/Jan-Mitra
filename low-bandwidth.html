Housing & Infrastructure

  • Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)
    Houses for all — Urban & Rural. Subsidy up to ₹2.67 lakh on home loans for EWS/LIG.
    Who: BPL families, no pucca house, income < ₹18 lakh/year
    Check Eligibility Document Checklist Ask AI
  • PM-KISAN
    ₹6,000/year direct income support to small & marginal farmers in 3 instalments.
    Who: All farmer families with cultivable land
    Check Eligibility Document Checklist Ask AI
  • Ayushman Bharat — PM-JAY
    Health cover up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary & tertiary hospitalisation.
    Who: ~12 crore poor & vulnerable families
    Check Eligibility Document Checklist Ask AI
  • National Scholarship Portal (NSP)
    Pre & post-matric scholarship for SC/ST/OBC/Minority/EWS students from Class 1 to PhD.
    Who: Students with family income < ₹2.5–8 lakh depending on category
    Check Eligibility Document Checklist Ask AI
  • PM Mudra Yojana
    Collateral-free loans ₹10k–₹10 lakh for non-farm micro-enterprises under Shishu, Kishore, Tarun tiers.
    Who: Entrepreneurs, small business, artisans
    Check Eligibility Document Checklist Ask AI
  • Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
    Guaranteed monthly pension ₹1,000–₹5,000 from age 60. Government co-contributes 50% of contribution for 5 yrs.
    Who: 18–40 yr unorganised sector workers
    Check Eligibility Document Checklist Ask AI
  • Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)
    ₹5,000 maternity benefit in 3 instalments for first live birth registered against Aadhaar.
    Who: Pregnant & lactating women for first child
    Check Eligibility Document Checklist Ask AI
  • Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)
    Free LPG connection with ₹1,600 financial aid for BPL, SC/ST, PMAY beneficiaries.
    Who: Women from BPL households
    Check Eligibility Document Checklist Ask AI
  • Swachh Bharat Mission (Toilet Subsidy)
    ₹12,000 grant for construction of individual household toilets in rural areas.
    Who: BPL households, SC/ST, small & marginal farmers without toilet
    Check Eligibility Document Checklist Ask AI
  • Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana (Saubhagya)
    Free electricity connection to all unelectrified BPL households; ₹500 deposit for APL.
    Who: Rural unelectrified households
    Check Eligibility Document Checklist Ask AI
