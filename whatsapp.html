FAQ

Common questions

Is this service free to use?
Yes, JanMitra is completely free for all Indian citizens. There are no charges for using the WhatsApp or web chat. Standard SMS charges from your telecom operator may apply for SMS queries.
Which languages are supported?
JanMitra understands and replies in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Kannada, Malayalam, and English. Just type in your language and it will respond in the same language.
Does it work on 2G / slow internet?
Yes. WhatsApp works on 2G networks, and SMS works with no data connection at all. The responses are text-only and load very quickly even on slow connections.
Is my personal information safe?
JanMitra does not store your personal details. Conversations are used only to generate responses in real time. We do not share data with third parties. You never need to share Aadhaar, bank details, or passwords.
Can I apply for schemes directly through WhatsApp?
JanMitra guides you through the process and provides official portal links, but actual scheme applications happen on government portals (like pmkisan.gov.in, pmjay.gov.in, etc.). JanMitra helps you prepare and navigate — it does not submit applications on your behalf.
