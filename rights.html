Right to Information (RTI Act, 2005)

Ask any government office for any document, record, or decision — they must respond within 30 days

✅ Free for BPL cardholders  ·  ₹10 fee for others  ·  Response within 30 days
01
Identify the Department
Find which ministry or government body holds the information you need.
02
Write Your Question
State your question clearly and specifically. You do not need to give a reason.
03
File Online / Offline
Visit rtionline.gov.in for central depts, or submit in person to the Public Information Officer.
04
Pay ₹10 Fee
Pay by IPO, DD, or online. BPL cardholders are completely exempt — attach a copy of your ration card.
05
Wait 30 Days
The PIO must respond within 30 days (48 hours for life or liberty). Keep your acknowledgement slip.
06
Appeal if Needed
Unsatisfied? First appeal to the same department (free). Then to the Information Commission (no fee).
