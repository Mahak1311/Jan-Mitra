JanMitra is a free AI-powered guide that helps every Indian discover, understand, and access government welfare schemes — in minutes, not months.

Our Mission

No Citizen Should Miss Out on Benefits They Deserve

India runs hundreds of welfare schemes across housing, agriculture, health, education, and livelihood. Yet millions remain unaware or struggle with complex paperwork barriers.

JanMitra makes this simple — one free platform that speaks your language, checks your eligibility, and tells you exactly which documents you need.
