
🚀 JanMitra – AI Civic Assistant for Communities
Making opportunities, rights, and public resources accessible to every community.
JanMitra is an AI-powered civic information platform that helps citizens easily discover government schemes, jobs, legal rights, documents, and community resources through a simple and intelligent interface.
Instead of navigating multiple complicated government portals, users can simply ask JanMitra and instantly receive verified, easy-to-understand information.

🧠 Problem
Accessing public resources in India is difficult because:
Information is scattered across multiple portals
Many platforms are English-heavy and complex
Citizens struggle to find schemes they are eligible for
Rural users often face low internet connectivity
Awareness about rights and opportunities is limited
Millions of people miss opportunities simply because they cannot find or understand the information.
💡 Solution – JanMitra
JanMitra acts as a single AI-powered gateway to public information.
It provides:
Government schemes
Jobs and skill opportunities
Legal rights awareness
Document guidance
AI-powered assistance
All in one simple platform designed for accessibility, multilingual support, and community impact.
✨ Features
🤖 AI Chat Assistant
Ask questions like:
“PM Kisan ke liye kya chahiye?”
“What scholarships are available for students?”
JanMitra AI understands the query and returns relevant schemes with eligibility details.
🏛 Government Schemes Explorer
Browse and discover government schemes across categories:
Agriculture
Healthcare
Education
Housing
Financial support
Includes filters and eligibility guidance.
📄 Eligibility Checker
Users can enter basic details such as:
Age
Income
Occupation
Location
JanMitra then shows schemes they qualify for.
📑 Document Checklist
Before applying for a scheme, users can view:
Required documents
Application requirements
Step-by-step guidance
⚖️ Know Your Rights
Citizens can learn about:
Consumer rights
Women's rights
Labour rights
RTI (Right to Information)
Includes emergency helplines and legal awareness resources.
💼 Jobs & Opportunities
JanMitra provides access to:
Central government jobs
Banking jobs
Defence jobs
Skill development programs
Users can directly apply via official portals.
🌍 Multilingual Support
Supports multiple Indian languages including:
English
Hindi
Tamil
Telugu
Marathi
Kannada
Users can switch languages instantly.
🎤 Voice-First Interaction
Users can interact with JanMitra using voice commands, making the platform accessible for people with low digital literacy.
📱 WhatsApp Access
JanMitra can also be accessed through WhatsApp, enabling citizens to interact with the system without visiting the website.
📶 Low Bandwidth Mode
The platform is optimized to work even with slow internet connections, making it accessible to rural and remote communities.
