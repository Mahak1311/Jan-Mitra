Tips for Better Results

Speak clearly, get better answers

🔇
Reduce background noise

Speak in a quiet spot for best results. If there's noise around you, use the text input box instead — it works just as well.

📝
Be specific

Instead of just "scheme", say "PM Awas Yojana eligibility" or "scholarship for Class 11 OBC student". Specific questions get sharper answers.

🌐
Mix languages freely

Hinglish works perfectly — say "Kisan scheme ke liye kaun eligible hai?" and JanMitra will understand. No need to speak in pure Hindi or pure English.
